## The rule, now that both cases are known

The rule is not "always use the claim shape". A **ledger**'s rows are claims and must parse as claims
(F.94: a trailing count or prose in cell 1 makes them vanish). A **crosswalk**'s rows are dispositions and
must NOT parse as claims (F.95). Decide what the table is for, then pick the shape.
